# Rules and preferences for CVs

These rules are **hard rules**: the resume-writer agent follows them even when they contradict its
own prompt. Put everything that is specific to you here (exact titles, year counts, wording you
accept or forbid), not in the agent files.

## Honesty
1. Every claim must be backed by this knowledge base. No invented numbers or experience; leave out what you do not know.
2. Titles: <Title> (<Company A>), <Title> (<Company B>). <How to treat the level or seniority>.
3. Years: total ~<N>; <Language A> ~<N>; <Language B> ~<N>.
4. <Skills that appear in the skills list only, without a job or years>.
5. <Project>: allowed verb "<exact verb>"; never "<stronger claim you cannot back>".
6. <Anything that was proposed or only partly delivered: the wording you allow for it>.
7. <Role-specific skill>: listed only for <kind of vacancy>; never years or project claims.
8. <What you can and cannot claim about leading or managing people>.
9. No company-specific lines in the base resume (they belong in the tailored copy only).
10. <Rule about internal project or system names in the summary and in the experience bullets, for example whether to describe what they do instead of naming them>.
